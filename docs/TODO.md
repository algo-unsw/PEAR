### Literature search: found, not entered (27 November 2026)

Each item was read in the version named; it waits for a decision by Serge (a new parameter, version or derivation, or a known flaw).

- **New versions or parameters needed.**
  - Independent Set, maximum degree 5, 6, 7 (above).
  - Paired Domination by $\mathit{tw}$, $O(4^{\mathit{tw}} n^{O(1)})$ (Theorem 3, proof omitted), and by distance $d$ to a cluster graph, $O^*(4^d)$ (Theorem 5): Andreev et al., IWOCA 2024.
  - Minimum Fill-In and Longest Induced Path by vertex cover and modular width: $O^*(4^{\mathit{vc}})$ and $O^*(1.7347^{\mathit{mw}})$, Theorem 5 of Fomin, Liedloff, Montealegre and Todinca (arXiv 1404.3882v1; SWAT 2014, Algorithmica 2018).
  - Weighted Steiner Tree by $|T|$ with the largest weight: $O^*(2^{|T|} d)$ in polynomial space, Theorem 6.3 of Lokshtanov and Nederlof (STOC 2010); Steiner Tree has no weight parameter.
  - Maximum Common Subgraph by the vertex cover numbers: $O((24k)^k (n_1 + n_2)^{2.5})$ where $k$ bounds the vertex cover number of both graphs (Abu-Khzam, IPL 114, 2014, end of Section 3; Theorem 4 states FPT only). Needs a parameter such as $\max(\mathit{vc}(G), \mathit{vc}(H))$.
  - $s$-Plex by $n$ (and by degeneracy): Chang and Yao (Proc. ACM Manag. Data 2(1), SIGMOD 2024, Lemma 3.5 and Theorem 3.9) give $O^*(\gamma_s^n)$, $\gamma_s$ the largest real root of $x^{s+3} - 2x^{s+2} + x^2 - x + 1$ ($\gamma_2 = 1.7549$, $\gamma_3 = 1.8886$, $\gamma_4 = 1.9479$, $\gamma_5 = 1.9751$), below the $\beta_s$ of Wang, Zhou and Xiao that `k-plex--n--wang-zhou-xiao-2022` records as $2^{(1-\varepsilon_s)n}$; and $O(n (\alpha\Delta)^{s+1} \gamma_s^{\alpha} + m \min\{\gamma_s^n, n^{2s-2}\})$ with $\alpha$ the degeneracy. The bound language has no per-$s$ explicit root, so in its current form the record would look equal to Wang–Zhou–Xiao's: decide how to state it (versions for fixed $s$, or a named family of constants).
  - Cluster Deletion by twin cover number and neighbourhood diversity, and Weighted Cluster Deletion by vertex cover (Italiano, Konstantinidis and Papadopoulos, Algorithmica 87, 2025): parameters not defined for the problem.
  - Weighted 3-Hitting Set by the solution weight: $O^*(2.2470^W)$ (Fernau, TCS 411, 2010, Theorem 2) and $O^*(2.168^s)$ in polynomial space, $s \le W$ (Shachnai and Zehavi, arXiv 1407.2033v2, Corollary 11). The weighted version has no $k_w$ parameter (Vertex Cover and Edge Dominating Set have one, and the same paper's results for them are entered).
  - Subset Sum in $O^*(2^{0.5n})$ time and $O^*(2^{0.246n})$ space, Monte Carlo (Belova, Chukhin, Kulikov and Mihajlin, arXiv 2402.13170v3, Theorem 1.4), stated for integers of absolute value at most $2^{O(n)}$. Nederlof and Węgrzycki reduce general instances to that case by standard hashing (entered with $2^{0.249999n}$); applying the same step to Belova et al. combines two results.
  - Max 2-CSP by $n$ and the domain size $r$: Williams (ECCC TR04-032, Theorem 3.1, the paper already entered for Max 2-SAT and Max Cut) counts satisfied constraints in $O(m^2 (3 r^{n/3})^{\omega})$, i.e. $O^*(r^{\omega n/3})$. It counts 0/1 constraints; PEAR's Max 2-CSP allows integer scores that are not polynomially bounded, and simulating them by multiplicities costs a factor polynomial in the scores. Decide whether it fits the version as defined (or add a version with polynomially bounded scores).
  - Partition into Triangles in $O^*(1.7549^n)$: Kloks, "A note on triangle partitions", arXiv 1203.5464v1 (2012, unrefereed, Theorem 1), a sharper analysis of Koivisto's dynamic program (entered: $O^*(1.769^n)$). Its Lemma 2 (a maximisation with $\gamma \approx 0.56985$) is unchecked.
- **Statements that combine two results** (Treewidth and Minimum Fill-In, Weighted 3-Hitting Set, monotone local search and the counting rule were added on 27 November 2026 at Serge's request).
  - Bounded-Degree Vertex Deletion with $d = 2$ by $n$: $O(1.7347^{n})$ from Corollary 1 of Fomin, Todinca and Villanger (arXiv 1309.1559v1; SICOMP 2015) with $t = 2$ and the CMSO property "maximum degree at most 2"; the paper does not name the problem.
  - #Set Cover in $O(1.22670^{m+n})$ and exponential space: stated inside the proof of Theorem 6 of the ESA 2009 technical report of Nederlof, van Rooij and van Dijk (UU-CS-2008-043), not as a theorem.

  - Chordal Vertex Deletion by $n$: $O^*(2^{\lambda n})$ for an unspecified $\lambda < 1$ (Bliznets, Fomin, Pilipczuk and Villanger, Algorithmica 2016, Theorem 1; exponential space). The bound language would need an unspecified constant below 1.
